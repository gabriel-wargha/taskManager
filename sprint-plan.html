Getting the cards into Trello

Create the whole board automatically

Run npm start in the taskManager folder on your computer, enter your Trello key and token, and pick a sprint. It creates each card in its owner’s To do list, with one shared Done list for the team, plus checklists and due dates. Press Download for Trello script first to bring your edits from this page. Your key never goes into this page.

cd ~/Desktop/taskManager
npm start

Or add cards by hand

A whole sprint at once

  1. Press Copy titles on a sprint.
  2. In Trello, open Add a card on your list and paste.
  3. Trello offers to create one card per line. Accept it.
  4. Open each card here and copy its description in.

One card, fully filled in

  1. Click a story to open it.
  2. Press Copy for email.
  3. Send it to your board's email address (board menu, Email-to-board). The first line is the subject.
  4. The subject becomes the title, #labels and @usernames are applied, and the body becomes the description.

Everything as a spreadsheet

Download the CSV for all cards or one sprint. It has title, description, labels, members, due date, sprint, week and branch, ready for a spreadsheet or a Trello CSV import tool.

Make labels named Backend, Frontend, Design and QA on your board first so the tags match.

Want the cards created for you? Run the taskManager app on your computer: it creates each sprint’s cards in each person’s To do list.
